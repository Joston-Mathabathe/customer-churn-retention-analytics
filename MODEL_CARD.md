# Model Card

## Models
Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest.

## Intended use
Portfolio demonstration of customer churn classification and business decision support.

## Data
Public IBM Telco Customer Churn sample dataset.

## Target
Churn: Yes/No.

## Limitations
- Public sample dataset.
- Fictional telecom company.
- Historical/static data.
- No guarantee that patterns generalise to another company or period.
- Model probabilities are not automatically suitable for operational use.

## Responsible use
Any real-world churn system should include data governance, privacy protections, fairness testing, model validation, monitoring and human oversight.
