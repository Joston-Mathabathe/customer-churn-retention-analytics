# Business Analysis

## Business objective
Use customer data to understand churn patterns and demonstrate how analytics can support retention planning.

## Stakeholders
- Customer retention teams
- Marketing teams
- Customer service
- Product teams
- Finance
- Senior management
- Data/analytics teams

## KPIs
- Overall churn rate
- Churn rate by contract
- Churn rate by tenure
- Churn rate by service
- Average monthly charges
- Average tenure
- Customer count
- Model recall and ROC-AUC

## Decision support
The analysis can help a retention team identify customer groups that warrant investigation. For example, the team could examine contract, tenure, service and billing patterns before deciding what retention actions are appropriate.

## Business Management contribution
Business Management knowledge helps connect the model to:
- customer retention objectives
- revenue protection
- service strategy
- customer experience
- cost of retention activity
- stakeholder communication

## Important
Observed associations are not proof that a factor causes churn. The dataset is a public sample representing a fictional telecom company.
